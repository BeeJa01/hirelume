For recruiters

How do I create a job and share it?

Go to Create job, add a title, a description and your requirements, then choose Save job and get link. You can copy the link, or share it straight to WhatsApp, LinkedIn or X.

Applicants don't need an account to apply.

How are applicants scored?

Every CV is compared with the requirements you listed. Required skills count double, and each requirement shows whether it was met, with evidence from the CV. The score always comes with a reason.

Scores usually appear within a minute of an application. A score shows as "Analysis pending" until then.

Can the AI get it wrong?

Yes. HireLume ranks and explains, but nobody is hidden or removed from your list, and only you can shortlist or reject. Always check the CV before you decide.

How do I shortlist or reject an applicant?

Open Applicants, choose a job, then click an applicant. Use the Shortlist, Reject or Undecided buttons at the top of their page. Use the tabs on the list to filter by status.

Why can't I edit my requirements?

Requirements lock after the first application so that earlier scores stay fair. You can still edit the title and description at any time. If you need different requirements, create a new job.

How do I close or reopen a job?

On Jobs, open the ··· menu on the job and choose Close Job. You can also use the Close job button on the job's applicants page.

Once closed, the job link shows "This job is closed" and nobody can apply. Your applicants and their rankings are kept. To open it again, use the same menu and choose Reopen job.

What is Blind Mode?

When Blind Mode is on, a CV opens as an anonymised text version instead of the original file, so you can review it without personal details.

What do applicants see after they apply?

If you turned on Let applicants see feedback when creating the job, they can see guidance on their own CV: strengths, gaps and tips. They never see their rank, how others compare, or your decision.

For job seekers

How do I check my fit for a job?

Go to New analysis. Add the job by pasting the text, uploading a screenshot or pasting a link, then add your CV and choose Analyse my fit. For the best match, paste the full job post.

What do I get from an analysis?

A match score with the reason behind it, whether each requirement is met, partly met or not found, your gaps, CV tips and next steps, plus 8 to 10 interview questions. Past results are saved in My analyses.

Which CV files can I upload?

For an analysis you can upload a PDF, an image or a Word file, up to 5 MB, written in English. When you apply to a job, the CV must be a PDF or Word file up to 5 MB. If a file can't be read, make sure it's a real PDF or Word document and not a renamed file.

Why does it say I've used all my analyses?

There is a daily limit on analyses. It resets at midnight, so you can try again tomorrow.

Is my information safe?

For an analysis, only your CV content and the job text are sent to the AI. Your email, phone number and address are never sent.

I applied to a job. How do I see my result?

You can apply without an account. To see your result later, create an account or log in using the same email address you applied with.

Will the recruiter see how I compare with others?

The recruiter reviews applications and makes the decision. You won't see how other applicants compare, and the AI guidance you get can be wrong, so treat it as a guide.

Account

I forgot my password

On the log in page, choose Forgot password?, enter your email and we'll send you a reset link. The link works once and expires after about an hour. After you set a new password, log in with it.

The reset email hasn't arrived

It can take a minute. Check your spam folder, and make sure you used the email you signed up with. If the link says it has expired, request a new one from the forgot password page.

Can I change my account type later?

No. You choose recruiter or job seeker when you sign up and it can't be changed later. If you need the other type, create a new account with a different email.

How do I log out?

Use the log out icon next to your name at the bottom of the sidebar, or choose Log out in the menu on your phone.
