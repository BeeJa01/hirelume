How it works

From a new job to a ranked list in minutes

Three steps for recruiters. Three steps for job seekers.

For recruiters

  1. 1

    Create a job

    Add the title, the description and your requirements. Mark each one Required or Nice-to-have.

  2. 2

    Share one link

    Post it on WhatsApp, LinkedIn or X. Applicants apply from their phones with no account.

  3. 3

    Review ranked applicants

    See every applicant best first, with a reason for each score. Then shortlist or reject.

For job seekers

  1. 1

    Add the job

    Paste the job text, upload a screenshot, or paste a link to the job post.

  2. 2

    Add your CV

    Upload a PDF, image or Word file. We only use it to compare it with this job.

  3. 3

    Get your match

    See your score, your gaps, CV tips and next steps for this job.

Our promises

Three rules that never change

People told us they would not trust an AI that decides for them. So ours does not.

  • The AI never rejects anyone

    It ranks and explains. Only the recruiter changes a status, and everyone stays on the list.

  • Every score has a reason

    See which requirements were met, partly met or not found, with evidence from the CV.

  • Guidance, not verdicts

    Applicants get strengths, gaps and next steps in plain language. Never a pass or fail.

Built for Nigeria

Made for how Nigerians hire and apply

  • Phone first

    Every screen works on a phone. Most job seekers apply from one.

  • Link friendly

    One link that works in WhatsApp groups, LinkedIn posts and more.

  • Local qualifications

    HND, OND, NCE, B.Sc. and NYSC are understood and never penalised.

  • No account needed

    Applicants apply with no account. Recruiters see every applicant ranked in one place.
